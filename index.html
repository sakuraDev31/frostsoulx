THE EXPERIENCE

A player that treats
listening as a place.

FrostSoulX keeps the interface calm while giving the music room to breathe. The visual system follows the artwork, the mini-player stays close, and discovery is woven into the listening flow.

Artwork-led playback

Artwork shapes player backgrounds and ambient visual treatment instead of sitting in a tiny square.

Discovery that remembers

Home, recommendations, search, history, artists, albums and playlists form one connected discovery loop.

Lyrics in sync

Timed lyrics, offsets, translation support and artist navigation keep the words close to the track.

A real music library

Local files, playlists, liked songs, downloads, cached playback and Android media controls are first-class features.

IMMERSIVE AUDIO LAB

Make the room
part of the track.

FrostSoulX is evolving a native, low-latency spatial-audio path for room-aware listening. The project explores physical environments, source positioning, stereo processing and real-time DSP rather than treating spatial sound as a single preset.

BathroomLiving roomHallSubwayTunnelCarOpen roadCaveStadium
Immersive audio is experimental and continues to evolve across playback paths and devices.
