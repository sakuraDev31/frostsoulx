The listening experience

Made for the feeling of a track.

Less time fighting menus, more time inside the music. FrostSoulX brings playback, discovery, lyrics, and audio experimentation into a single Android experience.

◈

Artwork in focus

A dark, expressive player where album art sets the mood and the interface stays out of the way.

Your music, locally

Play tracks stored on your device and move between songs, albums, artists, and playlists.

▤

Lyrics that follow

Time-synchronized lyrics help keep the words and the music in step.

⌁

Discovery for your mood

Inside FrostSoulX

Every part of the listening flow.

Real in-app screens, arranged to show the experience from discovery to playback. Tap any image to view it at full size.

Experimental · In development

Shape the space around your sound.

FrostSoulX explores immersive processing, custom room responses, spatial movement, and stereo bass controls. Effects and performance can vary by device and headphones. Standard playback remains available when you want the original stereo presentation.
