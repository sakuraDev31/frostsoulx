🎵 FrostSoulX

An artwork-driven Android music player with local media, YouTube Music discovery, synchronized lyrics, contextual recommendations, and experimental immersive-audio path.

🎨 Artwork-Driven

Beautiful UI centered around album artwork with dynamic color extraction

🎵 Local Media

Full support for playing local music files on your device

🎶 YouTube Music

Discover new music and access YouTube Music integration

📝 Synchronized Lyrics

Display lyrics that sync with the music playback

🎯 Smart Recommendations

Get contextual music recommendations based on your taste

🔊 Immersive Audio

Experimental immersive audio engine for enhanced sound experience
